How is the data handled?

The uploaded deck, event, and anonymous audience responses are deleted together after seven days. Responses may be used to operate and improve Call-in. Decks are not used for training and their copyright remains yours. Do not enter sensitive data.

By creating an event, you acknowledge the data use above.

Your deck disappears

An uploaded PDF is stored only for this event and automatically deleted after seven days. Call-in does not train on your deck or claim its copyright. Your copyright remains yours.

◎

Responses are recorded

Events created through call-in.mashbean.net record anonymous audience responses to operate and improve the project. Event, deck, and response data are deleted together after seven days. Do not enter sensitive or confidential information.
