Frequently asked

Before you start,
you may be wondering.

Call-in does not replace your slide software. It adds a live audience response layer to the deck you already use.

Does the audience need to install an app?

No. Audience members scan the QR code in a phone browser to report understanding, react, and ask questions.

Can I keep using my existing slides?

Yes. Upload a PDF up to 20 MB or paste a public Google Slides, Canva, or web-deck URL.

How long does Call-in keep slides and responses?

Hosted events, uploaded PDFs, and anonymous audience responses are deleted together after seven days. The public demo resets daily.

Can I self-host Call-in?

Yes. Call-in is open-source under Apache-2.0 and can be deployed from GitHub to your own Cloudflare account.
